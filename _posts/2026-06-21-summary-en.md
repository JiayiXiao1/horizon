## [3D Fiber Micro-Tweezer Achieves 100,000x Force Boost](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-06/19/content_534836.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Chinese researchers from Anhui University and the University of Science and Technology of China have developed a 3D fiber micro-tweezer using femtosecond laser composite fabrication, achieving an output force over 100,000 times greater than traditional optical tweezers. The device integrates light transmission, photothermal conversion, material response, and mechanical output on a single commercial optical fiber, enabling precise, low-damage, programmable 3D manipulation of microscale objects. This breakthrough overcomes fundamental limitations of traditional optical tweezers, which are weak and cannot manipulate opaque objects, and mechanical micro-grippers, which lack precision in confined spaces. The technology opens new possibilities for single-cell biology, microsurgery, and precision sampling in narrow environments, potentially transforming biomedical research and minimally invasive medicine. The micro-tweezer achieves continuous force control by adjusting input light power, and can perform precise sampling in spaces as small as hundreds of micrometers. The research was published in the journal Nature.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 20, 15:19

**Background**: Traditional optical tweezers use a focused laser beam to trap and manipulate microscopic particles, but they rely on transparent objects and produce weak forces (typically piconewtons). Mechanical micro-grippers offer stronger forces but are bulky and lack precision in tight spaces. Femtosecond laser fabrication uses ultra-short laser pulses to create precise microstructures with minimal heat damage, enabling the integration of multiple functions into a single fiber.

## [IETF Proposes HTTP QUERY Method for Safe Queries with Body](https://httpwg.org/http-extensions/draft-ietf-httpbis-safe-method-w-body.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The IETF HTTP Working Group is developing a draft to add a new HTTP QUERY method that allows safe, idempotent queries with a request body, similar to POST but with GET's safety and idempotency properties. This proposal addresses real-world limitations of long query strings in URIs, enabling more complex queries without hitting length limits, and improves caching, retry, and interoperability for web APIs. The QUERY method defines an Accept-Query response header for servers to declare supported query formats (e.g., application/graphql, application/sql). The current draft expires in December 2026 and is still under discussion.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 20, 06:28

**Background**: HTTP GET requests are safe and idempotent but cannot carry a request body, forcing complex queries into the URI, which has length limits. POST requests can carry a body but are not safe or idempotent, making caching and retry difficult. The QUERY method combines the benefits of both.
