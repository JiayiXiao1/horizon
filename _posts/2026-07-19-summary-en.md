At the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference, Bilibili showcased Project N.E.K.O. (Cat Girl Plan), an open-source AI companion that actively understands desktop content and initiates conversations. The project has gained over 1,000 GitHub stars and 10,000 Steam users. This project represents a novel approach to AI companions by combining proactive dialogue, desktop understanding, and local data storage, addressing privacy concerns while offering multimodal interaction. It could influence the development of open-source AI companions and community-driven AI ecosystems. The system supports Live2D and VRM rendering engines for character models, includes a physics feedback engine and TTS with VoiceClone voice cloning, and uses screen capture and visual multimodal technology to understand desktop content. Its architecture separates UI, AI brain, and memory, allowing core data to remain local.

## [Cambodia's flag carrier orders 20 C909 jets, a first for Chinese aircraft](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/china/story20260718-9384413) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On July 17, 2025, Cambodia Angkor Air signed a deal with COMAC to purchase 20 C909 (formerly ARJ21) regional jets, becoming the first foreign flag carrier to place a bulk order for Chinese-made commercial aircraft. The first deliveries are scheduled for the second half of 2026. This deal marks a significant milestone for China's aviation export ambitions, demonstrating that Chinese aircraft can gain traction with foreign flag carriers. It could pave the way for more international sales of COMAC's C909 and future models like the C919. The C909 is a 78–97 seat regional jet with a range of 2,225–3,700 km, originally known as the ARJ21 Xiangfeng. Cambodia's Prime Minister Hun Manet attended the signing ceremony in Shanghai and pledged government support for the aircraft's operation.

**Background**: COMAC (Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China) is a state-owned aerospace manufacturer established in 2008 to develop China's own commercial aircraft. The C909 (ARJ21) is its first regional jet, certified in 2014, and has been primarily operated by Chinese airlines. A 'flag carrier' is an airline designated by a government as its national carrier, often flying the national flag.
