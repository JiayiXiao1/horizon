## [Chinese-led BESIII Collaboration Confirms Existence of Glueballs](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 6, 2025, the BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists, announced the first experimental confirmation of glueballs, a new form of matter, after 15 years of research. They determined that the particle X(2370), discovered in 2011, has properties consistent with a glueball, including its quantum state and flavor-singlet nature. This is a landmark discovery in particle physics, as glueballs were predicted by the Standard Model but had never been observed before. It validates theoretical predictions and opens new avenues for studying the strong interaction and the nature of matter. The team used data from the Beijing Electron-Positron Collider's BESIII detector, collecting 10 billion J/ψ decays, which is 100 times larger than previous samples. They measured X(2370)'s spin-parity as 0-+ and identified multiple new decay modes, confirming its glueball nature.

This security fix is critical for Datasette instances that serve a mix of public and private tables, as it prevents unauthorized read access to sensitive data. The vulnerability affects instances where public and private tables coexist in the same database with permissions configured via the Datasette permissions system. Administrators are advised to disable the execute-sql permission on such databases as a mitigation, but the bug bypassed that restriction.
